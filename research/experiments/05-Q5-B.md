# Experiment 05-Q5-B — Israel unemployment next month, Approach B (ARGO-style breadth, Hebrew expansion)

Date: 2026-09-17 · Script: `research/experiments/scripts/05-Q5-B.py` · Runtime 791 s (13 min, dominated by ElasticNetCV) ·
GT requests: **36** (8 for related-query expansion incl. 3 rate-limit retries, in `scratch/q5b_expand_probe.py`; 28 interest pulls incl. 3 retries for 25 new terms)
Results: `research/experiments/05-Q5-B.json` · Sibling cell (hand-picked Hebrew terms): `05-Q5-A.md`

Question (Hebrew): "מה יהיה שיעור האבטלה בישראל בחודש הבא?" — What will Israel's unemployment rate be next month?

## 1. Setup

**Truth series** — identical to cell A: CBS Labour Force Survey unemployment rate, ages 15+, seasonally adjusted, %,
monthly, 2012-01 → 2026-07 (175 obs), spliced old definition (sid 491094, 2012–2024) + new definition (sid 41097, 2025–).
Loaded from the cache written by cell A (`truth._load("cbs_unemployment_rate_il", None)`; source URLs documented in `05-Q5-A.md`).
Last print 2026-07 = 3.1 %. GT extends to 2026-08, so a genuine h=0 nowcast of August 2026 exists.

**Query generation (ARGO-style breadth)** — seeds `["אבטלה", "דמי אבטלה", "דרושים", "פיטורים", "עבודה"]` →
`gt.expand_queries(seeds, geo="IL", timeframe=gt.timeframe_for("M"), max_per_seed=12)` → **56 distinct Hebrew terms**
(5 seeds + up to 12 top related queries each; every seed returned 25 top and 22–25 rising queries — Hebrew related-query
expansion works fully in `geo=IL`).

Did the expansion return useful terms? **Yes, for the unemployment-flavoured seeds.** `אבטלה` and `דמי אבטלה` yielded the
whole benefits-claiming funnel — eligibility (`אבטלה זכאות`, `זכאות דמי אבטלה`), the bureau (`לשכת אבטלה`, `לשכת התעסוקה`),
the act of reporting (`לחתום אבטלה` = "sign for unemployment"), forms and calculators (`טופס אבטלה`, `חישוב אבטלה`,
`מחשבון אבטלה`), payment (`תשלום דמי אבטלה`) — plus National Insurance variants. `פיטורים` yielded the dismissal-process
vocabulary (severance `פיצויי פיטורים`, dismissal letter `מכתב פיטורים`, pre-dismissal hearing `שימוע לפני פיטורים`,
advance notice `הודעה מוקדמת`, resignation `התפטרות`). `דרושים` yielded job-search behaviour (`דרושים עבודה`, `דרושים עובדים`,
`לוח דרושים`, `פורטל דרושים`) but also five regional splits and a brand. `עבודה` ("work") was the weakest seed: 6 of its
12 related queries were off-topic (worksheets, tools, working hours, social work, labour law).
Rising queries (not used) were COVID-era terms (`אבטלה קורונה`, `חלת`, `חלת דמי אבטלה`) and brands (alljobs, jobmaster).

**Curation** — 26 of the 56 expansion terms dropped, leaving 30 + the language-neutral topic `/m/07s_c` = **31 candidates**:

`gt` now strips quote marks/gershayim automatically (`_sanitize`), so no `חל"ת`-type breakage occurred; Google renamed no columns.

**Privacy-zero filter** (individual monthly pulls 2004–2026, `min_nonzero_share=0.5`): **29 of 31 survived**; dropped
`תשלום דמי אבטלה` (non-zero share 0.327) and `שימוע לפני פיטורים` (0.342) — both long multi-word phrases. Over the sample
actually used (2012–) 22 of the 29 kept series are 100 % non-zero and the lowest is 0.955 (`זכאות דמי אבטלה`); the
2004–2011 years are where the zeros live (lowest 2004– shares: מחשבון אבטלה 0.63, חישוב אבטלה 0.67, מענק עבודה 0.67,
טופס אבטלה 0.68, לחתום אבטלה 0.69). So expansion terms are noticeably thinner than the hand-picked ones of cell A
(all ≥ 0.90 from 2004) but still usable at monthly resolution since 2012.

**Design** — aligned monthly sample 2012-01..2026-07 (175 months); 29 GT features × lags 0–1 = 58 GT regressors + 3 AR lags
+ 1 Fourier pair; `transform="diff"`; `min_train=48` → 127 rolling origins at h=0 (2016-01..2026-07), 126 at h=1.
Horizons h=0 nowcast (`y_known_at_origin=False`) and h=1 (`y_known_at_origin=True`). Views: full and
`exclude=("2020-03-01","2021-06-30")`. Preprocessing: `pp.raw_pipeline` and `pp.djorno_pipeline(period=12, halflife=1.0, cluster=False)`.
GT models (9): `lasso` and `enet` × {raw, djorno} × {expanding, sliding `window=36`} (LassoCV / ElasticNetCV with
time-series inner CV), plus `ridge+djorno` (expanding). Benchmarks: naive, drift (`ev.drift`), seasonal_naive, ar_diff;
bench per view = lowest-RMSE target-only model in that view.

Honest reading: **Hebrew breadth does not add real value.** The only positive number in the whole cell is
`ridge+djorno` (29 unclustered expansion terms, ridge shrinkage) at +3 % OOS R² in the full sample at both horizons, with
a significant Clark–West but an insignificant Diebold–Mariano (p 0.6–0.7) — a 0.003 pp RMSE difference (0.211 vs 0.214)

the sliding `window=36` variants are *significantly worse* (DM p 0.01–0.07 in several views), because 36 monthly
observations cannot support 60+ regressors even with CV-tuned penalties. Compared with cell A (8 hand-picked terms,
`ridge+djorno` OOS R² −0.015 / −0.027 full sample), the 29-term ridge is ~4 pp of OOS R² better in the full sample and
the same or worse in the excluded view — the difference is the COVID-era spike in benefits-funnel terms, not persistent signal.

## 5. Surprises / problems

1. **Hebrew related-query expansion works and is semantically rich** — 25 top + ~25 rising per seed, all in Hebrew,
   covering the benefits-claim funnel and dismissal law. The problem is not coverage but relevance of the target: the
   SA unemployment rate barely moves, so richer search vocabulary has nothing to explain.
2. **Privacy zeros: 2 of 31 dropped**, both 3-word phrases (`תשלום דמי אבטלה`, `שימוע לפני פיטורים`, non-zero share ≈ 0.33
   over 2004–2026). Seven more kept terms have zeros in 2004–2011 but are ≥ 95 % non-zero since 2012. Expansion terms are
   thinner than hand-picked head terms; the filter threshold of 0.5 over the full 2004– window is stricter than needed
   when the sample used starts in 2012 — the skill could evaluate the share over the truth's date range instead.
3. **Sliding window 36 is harmful here** (RMSE +5–17 % vs naive, DM p < 0.05 in several views) and unstable: LASSO returned
   an empty set at ~60–77 % of origins and occasionally 17–26 features. ARGO's `window=104` weekly ≈ 2 years works with
   ~10 regressors; 36 monthly rows vs 63 regressors does not.
4. **The `gt_helps=True (moderate)` full-sample verdict is a Clark–West artefact**: CW rewards a shrunken larger model even
   when the RMSE gain is 1.4 % and DM p ≈ 0.7. The rule requiring both views (full and shock-excluded) is what saved the
   forecast from being labelled "GT-based". The skill should print the DM p next to CW and require the gain to survive the
   exclusion view — as this cell's `winner()` does.
5. **Runtime**: ElasticNetCV over 63 regressors × 127 origins took 65–185 s per backtest (total 13 min). LassoCV 30–60 s.
   Fine for research; for the skill, ridge (2–3 s) or a capped alpha grid is preferable.
6. **Request accounting**: `_with_backoff` increments `gt.REQUEST_COUNT` per attempt, so the 3 rate-limit retries in
   each of the two live runs count as requests (8 not 5 for expansion, 28 not 25 for pulls). Trendspy issued its "too many
   429" warning three times per run; every retry succeeded (`info["failed"]` empty). `len(gt.catalog())` moved 218 → 249
   (+31) during the run although this process saved 25 vintages — other cells append to the shared catalog concurrently.
7. The script's own `requests` field (28) counts only the live calls in the final run; the expansion JSONs were cached by
   the probe (`scratch/q5b_expand_probe.py`, 8 live calls). `requests_total_incl_probe` = 36 in the JSON is the budget figure.

## 7. Recommendation for the skill

For Hebrew macro questions the skill *can* rely on `gt.expand_queries` in `geo=IL` — Google returns full Hebrew related
queries, and after a simple curation pass (drop reversed-word duplicates, regional splits, brands, and anything not
containing a labour-market word) 30 usable terms emerge from 5 seeds with 29/31 surviving the privacy filter. But for
"Israel unemployment rate next month" breadth buys nothing: the best of nine ARGO-style models ties the random walk by
0.003 pp RMSE in the full sample only (CW p < 0.02 but DM p > 0.6) and is below the AR benchmark once the 2020–21 furlough
period is excluded, while LASSO selects no feature at the latest origin. The skill should therefore (a) answer with the
