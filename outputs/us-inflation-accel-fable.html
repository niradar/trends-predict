Method

  • Target frequency M, 271 periods 2004-02 → 2026-08; Google Trends pulled individually per query (geo=US), cached as dated vintages.
  • Rolling-origin backtest (120 origins max, min_train=60), direct multi-step design, levels; preprocessing fitted inside each training window.
  • Benchmark = best target-only model among naive / seasonal naive / AR: ar. GT models: ridge+raw, lasso+raw, ridge+djorno, ridge+djorno_nocluster, lasso+djorno.
  • Verdict rule: Google Trends 'helps' only if OOS R² > 0.02 vs the benchmark and Clark–West or Diebold–Mariano p < 0.10.
  • Interval: rolling conformal from the last ≤60 out-of-sample residuals of the chosen model (nominal 90%).

Caveats

  • Google Trends values are relative search interest (0–100 per request), sampled and revised by Google; not counts.
  • Search attention reacts to news as well as to the underlying quantity; relationships drift (ARGO-style rolling refits mitigate, not eliminate).
  • Out-of-sample gains concentrated in shock periods do not guarantee gains in calm periods — see the robustness table.
